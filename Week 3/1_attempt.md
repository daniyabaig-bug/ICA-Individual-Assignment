2. Referee's Note: Two Averages, One Argument

O (Observations)

- The overall mean price is 202.17, whereas the median price is 192.25.
- The mean is higher than the median by 9.92.
- Oil B has the highest average price of 324.83.
- Staple A has the lowest average price of 111.17.
- Sweetener D experienced the highest first-to-last percentage increase of 19.41%.

E (Possible Explanations)

- The difference between the mean and median may indicate that some relatively high observations are pulling the mean upwards.
- Different items have different average prices because they represent different products with different price levels.
- The median may provide a better representation of a typical price when extreme observations are present.

C (Causal Claims)

The descriptive statistics alone cannot establish why prices increased.

We cannot conclude that inflation, supply shortages or any other economic factor caused the observed price changes without additional evidence.

3. Conclusion

Both the mean and median are useful measures of central tendency, but they serve different purposes.

The mean considers every observation and is sensitive to extreme values. The median represents the middle observation and is less affected by unusually high or low values.

Therefore, the median may provide a more representative measure of typical prices when extreme observations are present. However, reporting both measures provides a more complete understanding of the dataset.
